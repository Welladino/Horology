# Content planner

## When to use

Planning or drafting content: a content calendar, briefs for posts, newsletters, articles or landing pages, or draft copy for a specific piece. Not for choosing the market (use `horology-gtm`) or the overall marketing plan (use `horology-marketing-strategy`).

## Required context and locations

- Read [domains, folders and templates](../horology-research/references/locations.md) before choosing a domain or entity, reading context or saving anything.
- Follow the [presentation rules](../horology-research/references/presentation.md) when creating or filling documents. Each document carries its default visual (Plan: a milestone timeline or process flow, such as the content calendar) or a `No visual: <reason>` line in its Visuals section.
- Read [linking rules](../horology-research/references/linking.md) when saving outputs.
- Resolve the permanent Pipeline ID, the request and the domain or entity. Find the brand guide, the accepted marketing plan and earlier content for this business in its Agent reference folders, the graph and the Document Index. Search the graph before drafting so you don't repeat published pieces.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the goal, audience, voice and channel. If there's no brand guide or agreed audience, state the assumptions you're working from and ask the founder to confirm them.
2. For a plan: propose topics tied to the messaging pillars, the format and channel for each, a realistic cadence, and a dated calendar.
3. For each piece: write a brief (goal, audience, key message, proof points, call to action, length, sources) and then a draft.
4. Write real copy, not descriptions of copy. Match the brand voice; avoid generic marketing language. Note anything that needs a fact check or the founder's input.
5. Check drafts against the graph: flag near-duplicates of earlier content (a similarity of 0.90 or more) instead of presenting them as new.
6. Save plans and drafts from the closest template in the Templates tab, in the Agent output folder that fits, and register them in the Document Index with a reserved Document ID, following the register ID and ownership rules in the linking rules (one reserved ID per document).
7. Set the Pipeline item to Awaiting decision when the plan or drafts are ready for review.

## Decision and permission boundaries

- Don't invent product facts, customer quotes, results or statistics. Mark anything that needs checking.
- Drafts only: never publish, schedule, post or send.
- If the request needs a strategy decision first, stop and recommend `horology-marketing-strategy` or `horology-gtm`.

## Output requirements

- A content plan (topics, formats, channels, cadence, calendar) and/or briefs with drafts, each linked to the Pipeline item.
- A short report listing pieces drafted, assumptions to confirm and anything pending.

## Verification before finishing

- Read back documents and register rows; confirm identities, links and preserved cells.
- Check every factual claim in the drafts has a source or a "check" marker.
- Distinguish saved work from pending writes.
