# Marketing strategy

## When to use

Turning an agreed go-to-market direction into a marketing plan: who to reach, what to say, through which channels, in what campaigns, and how to measure it. Not for choosing the target market or first channels (use `horology-gtm`) or for writing individual pieces and calendars (use `horology-content-planner`).

## Required context and locations

- Read the current [Workflow Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1CdytxCa2c-M7KroQ05JO52_mVhogQ5J_acBhRDRsF5I/edit), [Pipeline Locations](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1aL678ACs97YpLIGrGU5LbpXHc7jQBxl64ED-sLyehvI/edit#gid=2040100904) and [Document Presentation Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1D2N9VJRCmBFJ4T9cUGH47zQUVqk99sjavpbbwYIYY8I/edit).
- Read [linking rules](../horology-research/references/linking.md) when saving outputs.
- Resolve the permanent Pipeline ID, the bounded question and the domain or entity. Find the accepted GTM direction and any brand guide for this business in the graph, the Document Index or the Decision Register. If there's no agreed direction, say so and recommend a GTM step first.

## Workflow

1. Restate the agreed direction: segment, positioning and first channels, with links to the decisions behind them.
2. Define the audience in usable terms: what they care about, where they spend attention, and what would make them act.
3. Write 3–5 messaging pillars, each with the claim, the proof behind it, and the objection it answers.
4. Choose the channel mix and campaigns: purpose, audience, message, format, cadence and rough effort for each. Prefer fewer, well-run channels.
5. State budget and time assumptions in ranges, with currency and units. Unknown is not zero.
6. Define metrics: one primary outcome, a few leading indicators, the review point, and what result would change the plan.
7. Copy the Plan template from the Pipeline Locations tab into the verified domain destination, fill it, and register it in the Document Index. Set the Pipeline item to Awaiting decision when ready; draft a proposed decision only when the requested workflow includes one.

## Decision and permission boundaries

- Don't invent audience data, results or budgets. Mark assumptions.
- Plans only: no publishing, posting, sending, ad spend or account changes.
- If the work drifts into choosing the market or channels from scratch, stop and recommend `horology-gtm`.
- The founder decides. Keep the plan proposed until an explicit choice.

## Output requirements

- A marketing plan: agreed direction (linked), audience, messaging pillars, channel and campaign plan, budget and time assumptions, metrics and review point, risks.
- A short report of what was saved and what's still needed.

## Verification before finishing

- Read back the document and register rows; confirm identities, links and preserved cells.
- Check every claim in the messaging pillars has its proof, or is marked as needing one.
- Distinguish saved work from pending writes.
